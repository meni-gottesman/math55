Past finals: blind practice

Real Berkeley exams from other semesters · Finals plan · Flashcards

Why these matter. You have never seen these problems, and the topic isn’t labeled. That’s the only honest test of whether you can do the final, rather than feeling ready because the material looks familiar.

Rules for every one: real time limit, real allowed materials (Math 54: one handwritten sheet; Math 55: nothing), phone in another room. Before checking, mark each answer sure / unsure. A wrong answer you were sure of is the most important thing to fix: log it in red.

Different syllabus: older exams sometimes include topics your course didn’t cover (e.g. Math 54 heat/wave PDEs, variation of parameters, Laplace; Math 55 topics outside Rosen 1–10). Skip those problems and say so on the page; don’t count them.
